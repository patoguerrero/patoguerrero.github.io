Patricio Guerrero


My work lies in the field of inverse problems, computational imaging and speech processing, particularly applied to reconstruction algorithms with numerical optimization tools. Currently I am studying the use of bilevel optimization, automatic differentiation and proximal algorithms for both imaging and speech applications.

Research interests


  • Inverse problems in imaging/speech; variational, Bayesian and numerical approaches
  • Convex optimization / Bayesian sampling / machine-learning-related methods
  • HPC implementations for imaging/speech: analytical and iterative algorithms
